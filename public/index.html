I.The Experience

Less a bar.
More an exhibition.

MUSE is an immersive world where fine art, theatrical cocktails and intimate light meet behind a single door.

Every drink is composed like a masterpiece. Every room glows like a gallery after closing. And as the evening deepens, the music rises to meet you.

iArt

Masterworks reimagined, on the walls and in the glass.

iiCocktails

Smoke, flowers and spun sugar, served as theatre.

iiiAtmosphere

Velvet, brass and candlelight. Nothing too bright.

ivNightlife

As the hour turns, the salon becomes a stage.
